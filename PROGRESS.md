## Where things stand

- **Done:** Pocket List 0.2.0 (Fabric and NeoForge, and working keys on 26.2; not released yet): the HUD checklist, the list screen, pinning items and recipes, break-down to ingredients, JEI and REI support, chest memory; works on 26.2 and 26.3 in one jar; CI runs the game test on its own, with JEI and with REI. Storage blocks without a screen (shelves, Espresso Flavoured's canisters and jars) count once used, read live through Fabric's item storage.
- **Broken:** Nothing known as of this writing. The game test is timing-sensitive on a slow computer; it now waits for given items to arrive.
- **Next:** Nothing planned beyond feedback; the pack carries v0.1.0, which has the shelves, canisters and jars change. Decorated pots don't tell players' games when an item goes in, so they're left out of the player docs; a pot you used counts only what your game knew at chunk load.
